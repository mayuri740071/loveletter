
# 💌 LoveLetter

> A digital way to give someone a romantic gift.

LoveLetter is a digital gifting platform that allows people to create and share personalized romantic experiences without requiring an account.

Users can create a **Love Letter**, customize a **digital Bouquet**, and create a **Playlist** to share with someone special.

The recipient receives a private link and can open the experience directly without logging in.

## ✨ Features

* 💌 Create and customize digital love letters
* 💐 Create personalized digital bouquets
* 🎵 Create playlists with up to 5 YouTube songs
* 🖼️ Add a personal image to a playlist
* 🔗 Share experiences using private links
* 📱 Share through WhatsApp, Email, Instagram and Facebook
* 📄 Download letters and bouquets as PDF
* ⏳ Private experiences expire after 7 days
* 🔐 No login required for sender or receiver

## 💝 Pricing

Individual features are free.

The complete experience containing:

**💌 Letter + 💐 Bouquet + 🎵 Playlist**

will be offered as a paid package.

Additional technologies such as React, Node.js, Express and MongoDB will be introduced later as the project grows.

## 🎯 Project Goal

LoveLetter is designed to feel less like a traditional website and more like a digital romantic gift.

The goal is to create a warm, personal and memorable experience from the moment someone creates a gift to the moment their partner opens it.
